The Atelier

Build Your Own

Choose an earth, a form and a finish. The atelier renders the piece as it would leave the curing ground, weighs it to the nearest ten grams, and prepares a certificate. Nothing is sent anywhere; the piece exists only on this page, which the studio considers a perfectly respectable place to exist.

Preparing your piece

A short while, at altitude.

  1. Sieving the earth, three passes
  2. Pressing, in a single sitting
  3. Resting overnight, facing east
  4. Curing at 2,940 metres

Accelerated for your convenience. In the studio this takes between nine and sixty-one days.
