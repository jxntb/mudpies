Frequently asked

Questions, answered carefully.

Twenty-two of the questions the studio is asked most often, and the answers it has settled on.

01 Materials

What is a MudPie made of?

Earth, and nothing else. Each piece is formed from one or more of the nine earths held in the studio’s library, wetted with rainwater collected the season before. There is no binder, no glaze and no pigment. Any colour you see is the colour the ground was.

Where does the earth come from?

From a small number of beds the studio has visited for years: a cave system it declines to name, a dry riverbed, the Red Country, and the forest floor above the cave mouth. Exact locations are not published. The beds are small, and we would like them to stay that way.

The expedition is described on the Process page.

Is the earth organic? Sustainable? Ethically sourced?

We make no claims about the earth that the earth would not make about itself. It is retrieved on foot, in small amounts, and logged by hand. Beyond that we prefer to let it speak for itself, which in our experience it does slowly, and mostly about the weather.

Why is Husk in the library, if it is not strictly earth?

This is an ongoing discussion. Husk was admitted on a provisional basis after Collection 05 and has been under review ever since. It is very good at rims, which has so far been enough.

02 Craft

Are the pies fired, or baked?

Neither. Nothing is fired, baked or glazed. The pieces are cured in open air at altitude and remain, technically, mud. This is deliberate: firing would make them ceramics, and the studio has nothing against ceramics except that it does not make them.

How long does a piece take to make?

The forming takes a single sitting of between one and four hours. The curing takes between nine and sixty-one days. The expedition that precedes both takes eleven. On balance, a while.

Why are some pieces cracked?

Because the sun at 2,940 metres has opinions. The studio suggests a surface; the weather decides what happens to it. Cracks that appear during curing are kept, recorded and, where appropriate, named. We have never filled one. We considered it once, for about a minute.

Do you use moulds?

No. A mould would make every piece the same shape, which is the one thing a MudPie should not be.

03 Commissions

Can I commission a piece?

In spirit, yes. The atelier lets you choose an earth, a form, a finish and a name, renders the result and issues a certificate of formation. The certificate is real in the sense that you can save it. The commission is honoured in the sense that we think about it fondly.

Is the ordering real? Will I be charged?

No, and no. MudPies is a work of fiction. Nothing on this site places an order, takes a payment or asks for personal details. The atelier remembers your last configuration in your own browser so it is there when you return; the Reset button clears it.

How long is the waiting list?

The ledger runs to a little over two thousand pieces, and the studio makes around nine a year. We do not hurry. You may wish to give your piece a patient name.

Can I choose where my earth comes from?

You may choose any of the nine earths in the library. You may not choose the bed, the day or the weather, which is the studio’s way of saying that some things are best left to the earth.

Why is there no price?

A price would imply a sale, and a sale would imply that a piece leaves on a certain day in a certain condition, which is more than the studio can honestly promise. Also, as above, none of this is real.

04 Care & keeping

Can I eat a MudPie?

We would prefer that you didn’t. The pieces are formed for contemplation. For questions of a culinary nature we refer you to a search engine; we have run the search on your behalf, and it is linked from the homepage.

Are MudPies waterproof?

No. This is, in a sense, the point.

What happens if it rains?

The piece returns to the earth it came from. The studio regards this not as damage but as completion, and records it in the catalogue accordingly — see No. 031, Low Tide. Completion is not covered by any warranty, because nothing is.

How should a piece be displayed?

Indoors, flat, away from radiators, sprinklers, enthusiastic dogs and anyone under the age of six, who will recognise it immediately and want to make improvements.

Is there a warranty?

Each piece is guaranteed to be made of earth.

05 The studio

Is MudPies a real company?

It is a real website about an imaginary studio, which is more than many studios can say. The pies are fictional. The attention is genuine.

Why mud pies?

Because nearly everybody has made one, and almost nobody was taken seriously while doing it. We would like to correct that, slowly, one piece at a time.

Who makes the pieces?

The Maker, one sitting at a time. The studio is kept by four people who prefer to be known by what they do: the Maker, the Keeper of Earths, the Weather Correspondent and the Archivist. More about the studio.

Can I visit the studio?

Visitors are welcome on days without rain, which is to say rarely, and with notice. The studio does not correspond by email; letters may be left under the third stone to the left of the door.
